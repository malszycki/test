EMBO post-doctoral fellowship  (https://www.embo.org/documents/PDF/Postdoctoral_Fellowships_guidelines.pdf)
- Egibility
	- No need PhD at application, but max 2 years after obtaining PhD (which makes it impossible to re-apply after obtaining one)
	- re-application after denial permitted once, only after substantial changes
	- First author paper accepted in principle (need copy of editorial letter to prove)
	- Mobility. In case of EMBC states additional things
	- Scientific scope: insightful, understanding biological mechanism, not only describing it!
	- disclosure of the AI usage
- Application process (Deadline 23.01.2026 results end of June)
	- Written in english without any special formatting
	- No Figures No Tables
	- Two reference letters and acceptance letter from receiving institute. Need to use institutional email addresses
	- Pre-screening takes six to eight weeks 
		- applicant CV
		- biological significance of proposed work
		- host lab
		- reference letters
	- single expert interview (90min, 30min presentation, 60min Q&A)
	- final selection
		- possible additional 7min interview for 'grey zone' applicants
	- Earliest start is 1st July (spring) / 1st January (autumn)
- Benefits
	- money
	- yearly EMBO fellows meeting in Heidelberg (useful?)
	- Embo fellows network online for courses, jobs and further networking
	- Within 3 years of start free EMBO Leadership course (useful)
