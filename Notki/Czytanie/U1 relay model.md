# the model
Basically it all revolves around U1 being attached to transcribing Pol II and SPT6 or not. It involves exon tethering through U1 (3'ss-bound), and it's displacement to stable pre-B spliceosome complex, when the new 5'ss bound U1 appears and associates with Pol II (like handling a running stick). 
At the 3'end there's no new U1 binding, which releases SPT6 to associate with CPA and let it recognize the 3'end of the RNA. 

The only problem is, that the splicing was often reported to finish before the downstream 5'ss is transcribed [@Zeng2022ProfilingLariatIntermediatesRevealsGenetic], which the authors mention themselves and comment, that some exons may not require relay to complete. Maybe it is because of the prevalence of recursive splicing? [@Wan2021DynamicImagingNascentRNAReveals] No clue what would be the mechanism though...
