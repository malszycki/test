# Transcription initiation and 5'capping
- starts with CTD unphosphorylated pol II binding to the promoter. CDK7 (TFIIH) posphorylates CTD Ser5 to initiate pol II into transcription, but it pauses about 20-30 nt into the gene due to NELF complex(recruited by SPT4/5). This pausing allows 5'capping to occur [@Yoon2026CoordinatingMRNAMaturationU1Relay]
